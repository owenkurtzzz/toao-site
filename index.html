      02How we run ads

      Ads are the fastest way to lose money in a service business. Also the fastest way to grow one.

      The difference is the ten seconds after the click. Most agencies own the click and nothing after it. We own the page it lands on, the phone it rings, the calendar it books into and the invoice it turns into. That is the only reason we are willing to run ads at all.

      Follow one ad dollar.

      Switch off any link in the chain and watch where the dollar dies. Every one of these is a system we run, which is why we can promise the chain holds.

      $The clickMeta or Google, a homeowner with a problem tonight.
      1The pageLoads before they give up. Says what you do and shows the number.
      2The callPicked up in one ring, 24 hours a day, by a voice that knows your prices.
      3The bookingA real slot on your real calendar, confirmed by text.
      4The invoiceSent when the job closes, chased politely until paid.

      Every link holds. The dollar you spent on the click comes back as a paid job, and the ad that produced it gets more budget tomorrow morning.

      Creative

      Made for the feed, not the brochure.

      We shoot nothing generic. Each ad makes one claim you can back, in your voice, aimed at one moment: the pump grinding at 6:40 PM, the green water before the party. Video and stills, several variants, all tested against each other from day one.

      • Every claim comes from your services and your prices
      • Losing variants are cut, winners get the budget
      • You approve every ad before it spends a dollar

      The daily loop

      Watched every morning by something that never gets bored.

      1. 06:00Pull yesterdaySpend, clicks and calls from Meta and Google, per ad.
      2. 06:05Match every lead to a jobWhich ad rang the phone. Which call became a booking. Which booking got paid.
      3. 06:10Cut and scaleAnything below the line for three days is paused. What books jobs gets more.
      4. 06:20Write the noteOne paragraph to you: what changed and why. No dashboard to log into.
      5. FridayThe weekly reportFive numbers in plain words. Spend, leads, calls answered, jobs booked, cost per job.

      Questions

      Straight answers.

      Do I need to be technical?

      No. You need thirty minutes, access to your calendar, and the truth about your services and prices. We handle the rest and explain it in plain words.

      Do I have to take all twelve?

      No. The audit ranks the gaps by what they cost you and we start with the first one. Most businesses begin with the phone or the site. Some never need more than two systems, and we say so.

      What does the voice agent sound like?

      A natural voice with a name, trained on how you actually describe your work. You hear a recorded demo answering a realistic call before it ever picks up a live one.

      Where does the private AI run?

      On a machine in your office or on a private server in your name. Your documents are indexed there and never sent to a public model. We can show you the network traffic to prove it.

      Do you run the ads or just set them up?

      We run them. Every morning the agent pulls yesterday’s numbers, matches each lead to a call and a booked job, pauses what is below the line and gives more to what booked. You get a one-paragraph note daily and five numbers on Friday. The accounts, the pixel and the data stay in your name.

      What does it cost to run ads with you?

      Two parts: the ad spend, which goes straight to Meta or Google from your own account, and a management fee we agree in writing after the audit, against what a booked job is worth to you. No spend hidden inside a fee, no minimum term.

      Will you run ads for me right away?

      Not if the page says “Not Secure” or the phone goes to voicemail after five. Traffic to a leak is the most expensive kind. We fix the chain first, usually within the first two weeks, then turn the ads on.

      Will you make up reviews or numbers for my site?

      Never. Every rating, year in business and licence number on your site comes from you or a source we can verify. If we can’t verify it, the block is removed, not guessed.
